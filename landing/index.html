How it works

One loop.
From test to plan — and back.

Every step says what it rests on — and where there is no basis.

  1. 01

    Measure

    Tests with protocol, timer and counter. A whole test day from one phone or as a kiosk on a tablet — even without a connection.

  2. 02

    Place

    Percentile in a named reference group, with source, sample and quality. Without a reference, KYDON says so.

  3. 03

    Check

    Better only above your own measurement error. Below it: day-to-day form. The noise band shows it at a glance.

  4. 04

    Train

    A plan from sourced rules, with source and review status. Every block ends with a re-test. You or your coach decides.
