The session of the day as a start card, what you missed and what comes next on a timeline — then your changes, your biggest gap and the next sensible test.

Testing

The right tests for your sport.

Test batteries per discipline with picture, protocol, timer and counter. A whole test day runs from one phone — or as a kiosk on a tablet at the station.

  • 130+ tests, each with its protocol
  • Kiosk mode and test-day planner
  • Measuring works without a connection

Performance profile

Where you stand — and how solid that is.

Your profile as a percentile of a named reference group. Next to it: how many core tests are covered, how old the oldest one is and what is still missing. No overall score that hides gaps.

  • References with the source named
  • Coverage and data confidence on view
  • Hatched means: not covered

Change

Better — or measurement noise?

Every change is checked against how much your own values scatter. Only above your threshold does KYDON say "better" — below it, it says: too little to tell apart from your variation.

  • Threshold from your own measurements, from four values
  • Measure the same way and the threshold drops
  • Proven changes go into the report
