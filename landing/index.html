Why KYDON

Sport has always been judged by feel.
The future is measured.

Performance diagnostics used to mean a lab, a sports scientist and a waiting list. Most athletes never get there — and most training decisions are still made on gut feeling. KYDON brings the rigour of the lab to the field tests you can run in any gym, on any mat, on any track.

  1. 01

    Measured, not guessed

    Every test comes with its protocol: setup, execution, what counts. So a result means the same thing today, next month and in another gym.

  2. 02

    Benchmarked against real data

    Results are placed against published reference values — with the source named. Where there is no sound reference, KYDON says so instead of inventing one.

  3. 03

    Progress you can prove

    Every change is checked against the test's measurement error. You see when you really improved — and when it was just noise.

  4. 04

    Yours, and private

    Works offline in the hall, stores on your device first and syncs only if you want. Designed for GDPR from day one.
